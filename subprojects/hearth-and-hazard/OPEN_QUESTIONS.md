# Hearth & Hazard open questions and roadmap

Resolve these eight source decision gates first. Recommendations below describe the draft, not selections made by Noah.

| Gate | Draft recommendation | Alternatives retained |
|---|---|---|
| Compatibility | 2024 content with light annotations | Strict drop-in; broader conversion |
| Action economy | Action/Bonus Action/Reaction/movement | Three-action; Standard/Move/Minor |
| Tone | Heroic characters in a consequential world, peril modules | Gritty; high-powered; equal multiple profiles |
| Environmental interactions | Five-step procedure and one-rider limit as proposed Core | Optional chapter; universal automatic riders |
| Morale | NPC morale, at most two checks per encounter | GM toolkit; printed traits only |
| Exploration | 10-minute rounds under pressure | All hostile sites; freeform optional procedures |
| Advancement | Adventure Marks for playtest | Milestone; XP; treasure; hybrid |
| Darkvision | Compatibility dial; limited vision for perilous play | Retain; replace; remove |

After decisions: build a small playable packet, test representative exploration/social/combat scenes, then address the dependent class, spell and monster gates. Do not begin twenty-level rewrites based solely on a foundation recommendation. Recover the earlier April comparative study if it becomes relevant; its contents remain unverified.
