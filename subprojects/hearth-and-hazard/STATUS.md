# Hearth & Hazard status

Last verified: 2026-10-23 (America/New_York).

**Stage:** recovered foundation specification awaiting foundational decisions.

**Active version:** System Design Draft v0.1, July 2026; Working reference whose mechanics remain Proposed. Original DOCX has 97 rendered pages. No later full revision or explicit answers to its decision gate were recovered.

**Latest completed work:** comprehensive foundation covering core tests, actions, combat, environment, exploration, social systems, magic options, advancement, monster design, language standards and research/glossary appendices.

**Confirmed purpose:** a modular 2024-based fantasy adventure rules framework with more predictable world interaction and meaningful noncombat procedures. The generation request and draft establish the intended investigation; they do not adopt every recommended default.

**Current work/questions:** Section 15's eight foundational choices, then class/spell/monster dependencies. No complete twenty-level class catalog, rewritten spell catalog or full bestiary is claimed.

**Next action:** resolve the eight “Must Decide Now” questions in Section 15 before investing in class, spell or monster rewrites.
