## April–July 2026 scope

Retrieved project history describes an April cross-edition modular-rules investigation and a July 28 request for an iterative rough design draft. The earlier April attachment could not be identified by a current filename/usable identity, so its contents were not inspected or reconstructed.

## July foundation v0.1

The recovered DOCX is titled “Fantasy Heartbreaker System Design Draft v0.1” with a July 2026 design date. Its creation metadata is July 28. It provides executable proposed procedures and explicit open decisions rather than a complete rewritten game. Its 97-page length was confirmed by rendering; selected pages were visually inspected for extraction context, not exhaustively proofread.

The draft retains alternatives instead of silently choosing a full rules conversion. Its Section 15 asks for eight early decisions plus later class, spell, monster and playtest gates. No later answers or complete revision were recovered.

## October 23 organization
