# Hearth & Hazard current design

Use the [complete foundation draft v0.1](versions/foundation-draft-v0.1.md). It is a searchable rendition of the original DOCX, including tables and appendices in document order. No mechanics were rewritten during conversion. The retained DOCX resolves any material conversion discrepancy pending an explicit correction.

The proposal keeps a familiar 2024 d20 character/action framework and concentrates additions on time/light, exploration, environmental interaction, morale, social consequence and GM procedures. It separates a proposed default from optional modules such as three-action combat, wounds, altered Darkvision, real-time torches, encumbrance, resting, roll-to-cast and advancement.

The draft's proposed default 10-minute Exploration Rounds apply under pressure. That is a rule in this draft, not a shared rule silently approved by Shadowdark's later conversation. The eight foundational questions in Section 15 remain a design gate.

**Approval boundary:** no complete mechanics package, optional module or class rewrite was found explicitly adopted. The document supplies a foundation for decisions and tests, not a finished standalone game.
