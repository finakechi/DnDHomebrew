# Bartitsu Rogue status

Last verified: 2026-10-23 (America/New_York).

**Stage:** provisional complete subclass; balance and wording revision pending.

**Active version:** October 5 Design Report, Section 6; Working, not finally approved. Complete source recovered from DOCX and rendered to a searchable Markdown rendition.

**Latest completed material recovered:** mechanical balance audit, live-formula workbook, reproduction ZIP and originality review. The audit's dated verdict is Slightly Overtuned with moderate confidence, concentrating concern on levels 13/17 and cumulative off-turn damage/control. No new independent recalculation or actual playtest occurred in this archive task.

**Confirmed direction:** preserve Rogue identity, cane/leverage/counter theme and meaningful off-turn Sneak Attack; do not add Monk-style Extra Attack, a scaling martial-arts die or a point pool. These are recorded working design boundaries, not proof of approval of every numeric feature.

**Current questions/work:** Perfect Leverage's 2d6 discount plus three effects; Counterstroke frequency; Practiced Cane/magic bonus stacking; unarmed/Finesse and mastery wording; control sizes/escape/save choices. Audit proposals remain unadopted.

**Next action:** review the audit's specific wording and level-17 options against the provisional baseline; select changes explicitly before editing a new version.
