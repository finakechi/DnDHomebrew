# Bartitsu Rogue open questions and roadmap

## Established review work

1. Decide whether to test Perfect Leverage with the written 2d6 discount or the audit's proposed 1d6 discount/three-effect alternative. Preserve both; C-004 remains open.
2. Resolve Practiced Cane stacking, damaging unarmed eligibility, mastery selection, Grapple/escape DC and duration wording in an explicitly identified revision.
3. Test Counterstroke with varied melee attention, reaction competition and actual off-turn Sneak Attack qualification; avoid assuming every enemy attacks the Rogue.
4. Test free level-9 and level-11 combinations and level-17 multi-effect control separately from weapon DPR.
5. Revisit Sap/Slow/Topple, concealment wording, Redirect and Suppress after core interactions are clear.

## Deferred alternatives

Turnabout, setting-neutral subclass naming and alternative counter restrictions remain options, not tasks already selected. The originality report is a lead register with known rules errors; a fresh audit would require current primary sources and bounded claims.
