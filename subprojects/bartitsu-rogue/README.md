# Bartitsu Rogue

A 2024 Rogue built around cane fighting, unarmed/improvised precision, leverage, deception and counterattacks. It should remain a one-good-hit Rogue using Sneak Attack and Cunning Strike rather than becoming a Monk with a new point pool.

The [October 5 design report](versions/provisional-design-2026-10-05.md) preserves the complete provisional subclass and its design history. Its “provisionally locked” label is a working baseline. The [balance report](archive/mechanical-balance-audit-2026-10-05.md) and [originality review](archive/originality-review-2026-10-05.md) are separate analyses; neither automatically revises the rules or proves originality.
