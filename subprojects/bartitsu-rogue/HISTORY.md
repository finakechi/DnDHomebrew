# Bartitsu Rogue history and changelog

## September 2026 research

The user requested historical/design foundations rather than a finished subclass at that stage. The [historical report](archive/historical-foundations.md) and its [cited original rendition](archive/historical-foundations-cited.md) distinguish the inspiration, but its older mechanical assumptions do not govern later 2024 rules. Original conversation chronology is partly retrieved summary; no complete transcript is available.

## October 5 provisional design

The [design report](versions/provisional-design-2026-10-05.md) records the progression: unarmed Sneak Attack → improvised tools → dedicated Cane → paired feint/reaction counter → broader Cunning Strike specialization. It preserves abandoned first-round/attitude surprise triggers, bonus-action Grapple/Shove, alternating-mode bookkeeping, Topple/Slow alternatives, Turnabout, and generic capstone ideas. Section 6 is the complete provisional baseline; Section 8 says “locked” means the next pass's baseline, not immunity from revision.

An [originality review](archive/originality-review-2026-10-05.md) reported no close full-package duplicate in its search, with coverage limits. The [mechanical audit](archive/mechanical-balance-audit-2026-10-05.md) then flagged cumulative level-13/17 power and several wording issues; workbook and reproduction assets were recovered. The audit also identifies specific rules inaccuracies in the earlier research. This organization does not resolve those by editing historical reports.

## October 23 organization

Preserved the original DOCX, workbook and ZIP in Drive; created one searchable rendition and archived reports in GitHub. The complete current rules remain unchanged. No tuning recommendation, playtest finding or final approval was added.
