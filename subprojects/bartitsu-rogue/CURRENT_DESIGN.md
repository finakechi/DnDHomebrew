# Bartitsu Rogue current design

The complete working mechanics are [Design Report Section 6](versions/provisional-design-2026-10-05.md#6-current-provisional-subclass), a Markdown rendition of the retained original DOCX. Use the full section for wording, saves, ranges and action costs.

| Rogue level | Working feature |
|---|---|
| 3 | Bartitsu Training: Finesse unarmed/improvised weapons, Dexterity Grapple/Shove DC, Create an Opening and reaction Exploit an Opening; signature Cane |
| 9 | Scientific Leverage: each Cunning Strike cost reduced by 1d6, minimum 0; Disarm, Redirect, Joint Lock and Suppress; Cane +1 |
| 13 | Counterstroke: counterattack as part of Uncanny Dodge; Cane +2 |
| 17 | Perfect Leverage: 2d6 reduction and up to three different Cunning Strike effects; Cane +3 |

The Cane uses 1d6 Bludgeoning, Versatile (1d8) and provisional Sap. Its Finesse comes through Bartitsu Training. This is the baseline as written, including unresolved magic-bonus and eligibility wording.

**Proposed, unadopted audit adjustments:** explicitly prevent Cane bonus stacking; clarify damaging unarmed/Finesse and mastery access; test level 17 with a 1d6 discount while retaining three effects; examine Counterstroke before deciding a restriction. These recommendations are preserved in the [audit](archive/mechanical-balance-audit-2026-10-05.md), not silently applied here.
