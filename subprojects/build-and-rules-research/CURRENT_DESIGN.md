# Research working briefs

There is no adopted mechanics document for this workstream. Use the constraints in STATUS.md and keep results separately labeled by brief and edition.

Future build records should state level, source eligibility, skill/tool benefits, combat costs and assumptions. Rules notes should distinguish official text, interpretations and edition differences. Adventure comparisons should link the publisher and state intended level, dungeon scope, danger style and duration assumptions.

These are recordkeeping requirements, not completed research findings. Do not claim that previous answers, full build alternatives or a purchased adventure were reviewed when they were not available.
