# Veteran Fighter history and changelog

## Early designs through August 2026

The [original timeline](archive/evolution-timeline-through-2026-08-27.md) preserves fourteen stages; exact dates for all stages are not established. A used broad attack-replacement Veteran Actions; B used a Tactics menu; C shifted toward enhanced named generic actions. D and D.1–D.4 evolved into self-contained Lessons, fieldcraft and readiness. D.4.1 received timing cleanup; D.4.2 emphasized concision. [D.4.1 text](versions/d4-1.md) and the D.4.2 PDF are retained; neither is today's testing build.

Post-D.4.2 review removed Vigilant Response, strengthened two-choice Readiness and simplified Veteran of a Hundred Fights. Finish the Wounded and the unresolved Eye feature were reconsidered. Change the Plan explored changing weapon/unarmed methods after failure. [The master handoff](archive/master-handoff-2026-08-27.md) records accepted, provisional and abandoned paths at that stage; it must not be applied as a timeless current design.
