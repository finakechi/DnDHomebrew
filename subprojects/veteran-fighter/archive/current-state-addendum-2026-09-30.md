# Veteran Fighter — Current-State Addendum
**Date:** 2026-09-30

This addendum records known decisions made after the August 27, 2026 Master Project Handoff. It should override older material where it directly conflicts.

## Accepted after the handoff

### Practical Experience
Practical Experience was accepted as the replacement for the unresolved Veteran's Eye / Fieldcraft direction.

Current concept:
- gain proficiency in an appropriate Fighter-facing skill; and
- gain Expertise in an appropriate Fighter-facing skill.

The exact eligible-skill wording should be taken from the latest relevant project chat if available.

## Still provisional / under active testing

### Change the Plan
The post-handoff direction remains the provisional weapon ↔ Unarmed Strike transition:

- once during each Attack action on the Veteran's turn;
- after missing a creature with a melee weapon attack, immediately make an Unarmed Strike against that creature; or
- after the creature succeeds on its save against the Grapple or Shove option of the Veteran's Unarmed Strike, immediately make a melee weapon attack against it;
- an attack generated by Change the Plan cannot trigger Change the Plan again;
- the generated weapon attack currently retains normal Weapon Mastery functionality for testing.

The core thematic intent is “whatever works”: if the proper technique fails, immediately change methods.

No accepted level-15 upgrade to Change the Plan is recorded.

## Battlefield Readiness

The accepted structural direction remains:
- at Fighter 7, choose two different Battlefield Readiness options at the start of the first turn in combat;
- Vigilant Response is removed/superseded.

Established options from the accepted package:
- Take Position;
- Sweep;
- Assess;
- Brace at Fighter 15.

Later discussion explored additional options:
- Counter-Ambush was regarded favorably;
- Press the Advantage was rejected as too powerful;
- Exploit the Confusion was proposed as the preferred replacement for Press the Advantage, but no explicit user acceptance is recorded in the retrieved context.

Treat Counter-Ambush / Exploit the Confusion as design-history candidates unless the actual later chat establishes a more definitive status.

## Retained

### Hard-Won Lessons
- current 14-Lesson roster retained for now;
- Lessons known: 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 at Fighter 3 / 7 / 10 / 15 / 18.

### Been Here Before
Retained in concept, including Recalled Lesson and rest-based adaptation. The structural concern remains that temporary access to niche Lessons may reduce the incentive to learn niche Lessons permanently.

### No Lesson Wasted
Retained in concept:
- broad Lesson rearrangement after rests;
- temporary qualified Lesson when Initiative is rolled;
- Battlefield Readiness gains Brace.

The obsolete Finish the Wounded damage-upgrade clause should not return.

### Veteran of a Hundred Fights
The simplified proactive level-18 direction remains:
- choose one visible creature at the start of each turn;
- become exceptionally effective against that threat for the round through the current advantage/disadvantage package.

The older multi-part Read the Threat / Punish the Mistake capstone remains superseded.

## Superseded / rejected material that should not silently return

- Finish the Wounded
- Vigilant Response
- Tactical Blow
- Applied Mastery
- the old attack-replacement / Veteran Actions engine
- broad extra-Reaction engines
- the old multi-part level-18 capstone
- Press the Advantage
- Field-Expedient Fighting as a core direction

Older material can still be mined for themes or isolated ideas, but not treated as current mechanics.
