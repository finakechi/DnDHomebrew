# VETERAN FIGHTER SUBCLASS — MASTER PROJECT HANDOFF

## 1. PROJECT IDENTITY

**Project / Topic:**  
**Veteran** — homebrew Fighter subclass for D&D 5.5e / the 2024 revised fifth-edition rules.

**Primary objective:**  
Design a mechanically strong, distinctive, mundane Fighter subclass representing an experienced front-line soldier, campaigner, survivor, scout, guard, mercenary, caravan hand, sailor, or practical battlefield problem-solver.

The Veteran should derive power from experience, fieldcraft, adaptability, practical violence, situational awareness, and accumulated tricks rather than magic, supernatural abilities, command authority, or a Battle Master-style maneuver resource.

**Current phase:**  
Active mechanical revision/prototyping after a substantial audit and wording pass.

The project is **not currently at a stable final-draft stage**. Several features are stable, two major high-level structural changes have recently been accepted, a new level-3 combat feature has just been provisionally selected, and Veteran's Eye is awaiting simplification/replacement.

**Handoff date/context:**  
August 27, 2026. The project has progressed beyond attached **Veteran.md / Iteration D.4.1** and beyond the displayed D.4.2 wording pass.

D&D Beyond now labels the updated 2024 rules as **5.5e**, while continuing to distinguish the 2014 rules as 5e; both “2024” and “5.5e” are therefore used in this project.

---

# 2. EXECUTIVE STATE SUMMARY

The Veteran is built around three broad ideas:

1. **Hard-Won Lessons** — selectable techniques that modify specific existing actions or Reactions.
2. **Core Veteran experience/adaptability** — preparedness, knowing what works, rapidly changing plans, and practical fieldcraft.
3. **Mundane martial competence** — strong enough to matter alongside other Fighter subclasses and high-level spellcasters without becoming magical.

The current architecture has recently changed substantially.

### Currently accepted / provisionally accepted

- **Hard-Won Lessons stays as-is for now.**
- The current 14 Veteran Lessons remain unchanged for now.
- **Finish the Wounded is superseded provisionally by Change the Plan.**
- **Vigilant Response is being removed.**
- Level 7 **Battlefield Readiness is being strengthened** so the Veteran chooses **two different Readiness options instead of one**.
- The old level-18 multi-part capstone is being replaced by a much cleaner proactive **Veteran of a Hundred Fights** that lets the Veteran choose one visible creature at the start of each turn and become exceptionally effective against that one threat.
- **Veteran's Eye is not satisfactory in its current form.** The user likes having a ribbon/fieldcraft feature at level 3 but considers the current version bloated and specifically dislikes Size Up being a Study-action enhancement that feels like it belongs in Hard-Won Lessons.
- The former level-15 **Finish the Wounded → 2d10** upgrade is now orphaned because Finish the Wounded is being removed. No level-15 upgrade to Change the Plan has yet been accepted.

### Immediate design focus

The newest accepted provisional feature is:

## Change the Plan

Once during each Attack action on the Veteran's turn:

- after missing a creature with a **melee weapon attack**, immediately make an **Unarmed Strike** against that creature; or
- after a creature succeeds on its save against the **Grapple or Shove** option of the Veteran's Unarmed Strike, immediately make a **melee weapon attack** against that creature.

An attack or Unarmed Strike generated by Change the Plan cannot trigger Change the Plan again.

**Current intentional playtest choice:** the follow-up weapon attack retains **full Weapon Mastery functionality**.

If this proves too powerful, the **first proposed pullback is to prevent the follow-up weapon attack from using a Mastery property**, rather than gutting the core transition mechanic.

This feature arose from a broader thematic realization: Veteran combat should embody **“whatever works,” “use the tool you have,” and “if the proper technique fails, immediately do something else.”**

Historical European martial material provides useful thematic precedent. Fiore de'i Liberi explicitly describes transitioning from cut to thrust, then hilt/pommel strikes, and switching to grappling when the sword is not effective.

The current design is promising but **not ready for a full rewrite yet**, mainly because Veteran's Eye and Change the Plan's level-15 evolution remain unresolved.

# 3. USER GOALS AND PRIORITIES

## Hard requirements

- The Veteran must remain a **mundane** Fighter subclass.
- It must not require spellcasting or pseudo-magical explanations.
- It must use the 2024/5.5e Fighter chassis correctly.
- Hard-Won Lessons should modify recognizable existing actions/Reactions rather than inventing a parallel “Veteran Action” system.
- Avoid turning the subclass into Battle Master with another maneuver-resource system.
- Do not casually give generic utility actions DPR-neutrality with the Attack action.
- Do not create loopholes around the Fighter's Action Surge restriction on the Magic action.
- Do not casually grant extra Reactions or repeated off-turn attacks without strong justification.
- Do not accidentally become Rogue, Ranger, Bard, Cavalier/Sentinel, Eldritch Knight, or Warlord/Banneret.
- Support **Unarmed Strike** builds where practical. Do not assume every Veteran must use conventional weapons.
- Preserve the distinction between **Attack action** and “make an attack.”
- Use current 2024/5.5e terminology and timing conventions.

## Strong preferences

- Veteran should feel like a person who:
  - has seen similar problems before;
  - knows when the technically proper method is wrong;
  - knows what breaks;
  - knows where to stand;
  - knows when an enemy is making a mistake;
  - understands routes, camps, fortifications, equipment, and practical people.
- Strong martial features are acceptable, including at high levels.
- High-level martial abilities should be evaluated in a world where full casters have 8th- and 9th-level spells.
- Utility should be meaningful at the table, not decorative ribbon text pretending to be agency.
- Campaign-dependent Lessons are acceptable **if they are genuinely powerful in campaigns where they apply**.
- Feat synergy is considered inherently good unless it causes genuine imbalance, mandatory optimization pressure, rules confusion, or unintended action compression.
- Use closed, bounded mechanics rather than open-ended “ask the DM if my dirty trick works” systems.
- Practical information should usually be qualitative rather than exact stat-block extraction.
- The user prefers features with a coherent fantasy rather than mechanically competent but generic bonuses.

## Soft preferences / nice-to-haves

- The Veteran should be capable of becoming a credible **second-best** party member for stealth, scouting, social interaction, sabotage, or magic-item-heavy play through selected Lessons.
- Lessons should allow different Veterans to feel meaningfully different.
- Out-of-combat competence should involve physical spaces, equipment, routes, human behavior, battlefield evidence, and practical knowledge.
- The subclass should avoid unnecessary bookkeeping.

## Explicitly unacceptable outcomes

- “Battle Master with more maneuvers.”
- “Rogue with Fighter numbers.”
- “Ranger without magic.”
- “Bard with soldier flavor.”
- “Cavalier/Sentinel lockdown subclass.”
- “Eldritch Knight without spells.”
- Commander/officer/warlord identity.
- A random bag of unrelated useful buttons.
- A third-level combat feature that is powerful but **boring/passive**, as Finish the Wounded became.
- A supposedly minor ribbon that consumes excessive word count or contains multiple separate mechanics, as Veteran's Eye currently does.
- An unarmed build being arbitrarily unsupported merely because Weapon Mastery does not apply to Unarmed Strikes.

## Priority order

1. **Distinct Veteran identity and satisfying gameplay.**
2. Correct 2024/5.5e rules interaction and clean action economy.
3. Strong enough martial power to remain relevant at all tiers.
4. Preserve meaningful build/campaign choices through Lessons.
5. Noncombat usefulness and second-best-role flexibility.
6. Concision and table usability.
7. Exact numerical tuning, which can be playtested after the mechanical identity works.

## Current 14 Veteran Lessons

### Level 3-accessible Lessons

**Forced March — Dash**
- Dash.
- +10 feet movement associated with the action.
- Difficult Terrain costs no extra movement until turn end.

**Break Contact — Dash**
- Dash.
- Choose one visible creature.
- Veteran's movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks from that creature for the turn.

**Field Concealment — Hide**
- Armor-imposed Stealth Disadvantage does not apply to the Hide-action check.
- On success, choose up to PB willing creatures within 30 feet that can see/hear the Veteran.
- For 10 minutes, Veteran and selected creatures have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made as part of Hide and ignore armor-imposed Disadvantage on those checks.
- Does not waive normal Hide requirements.
- The 10-minute benefit is once per Short or Long Rest.

**Shield the Line — Dodge**
- When Veteran Dodges, choose willing creature within 5 feet.
- That creature has Half Cover while remaining within 5 feet until start of Veteran's next turn and while Veteran is not Incapacitated.

**Hold Ground — Dodge**
- While benefiting after Dodge:
  - Advantage on saves/checks to avoid being knocked Prone or moved against will.
  - If forced movement permits no save/check, reduce distance by 10 feet, minimum 0.

**Fire-Team Assist — Help**
- Ability-check Help can reach an ally within 30 feet if communication is possible.
- Attack Help can target enemy within 15 feet instead of normal close range.
- If helped attack hits, attacker may immediately move 5 feet without provoking from the creature hit.

**Threat Scan — Search**
- Search to locate a creature gets Advantage if the target moved, attacked, or made a clearly audible sound since end of Veteran's previous turn.
- Success may reveal direction of recent movement/attack when evidence permits.
- If multiple creatures located, choose one.
- First attack by Veteran or an ally who can see/hear Veteran against chosen target before Veteran's next turn has Advantage.

### Level 7 Lessons

**Hair Trigger — Ready**
- Prerequisite Fighter 7.
- Veteran takes Ready and readies the **Attack action** using weapon/Unarmed Strike.
- When trigger occurs and Reaction is spent to take the readied Attack action:
  - make two attacks;
  - at Fighter 15, three attacks.
- Once during that readied Attack action, a hit deals +1d10 damage.

Important rules correction already established:
Ready can ready the Attack action, but Fighter Extra Attack only grants its additional attacks when the Attack action is taken **on the Fighter's turn**. A typical readied Attack action on another creature's turn therefore gets only one attack absent Hair Trigger. Hair Trigger explicitly creates its own 2/3-attack count.

Do **not** reintroduce the mistaken idea that Ready inherently gives the full Fighter Extra Attack sequence.

**Hook and Turn — Opportunity Attack**
- Prerequisite Fighter 7.
- On an Opportunity Attack hit:
  - +1d10 damage;
  - if target Large or smaller, move it up to 5 feet to an unoccupied space within reach.
- Movement need not be directly away.

### Level 10 Lessons

**Common Soldier's Tongue — Influence**
- Advantage on qualifying Influence checks involving people with substantial practical occupational experience:
  - military,
  - guard,
  - mercenary,
  - dangerous manual labor,
  - caravan,
  - shipboard,
  - comparable practical work.
- Approach must concern relevant practical matters such as danger, supplies, routes, pay, working conditions, security, fortifications, logistics, survival.
- No magical compulsion and no impossible Influence attempts.

**Battlefield Activation — Magic**
- Magic action specifically to activate a magic item, including one whose activation casts a spell.
- Can draw/stow the item as part of action.
- After activation resolves, can make one weapon or Unarmed Strike attack as Bonus Action that turn.
- Does not waive item requirements.
- Does not change activation action.
- Does not permit Magic action through Action Surge.

**Sabotage — Utilize**
- Advantage on Utilize checks to disable/jam/wedge/tangle/break/obstruct nonmagical object or mechanism.
- Reversible sabotage may require an action plus check to reverse:
  - DC = 8 + PB + Strength or Dexterity modifier selected by Veteran.
- Obvious bypass methods can automatically work.

**Quick Barricade — Utilize**
- Use nearby sturdy movable objects or suitable loose material.
- Form temporary barricade along edge of 5-foot space within reach.
- Grants Half Cover through protected edge.
- No supernatural/special durability.

**Find the Weak Point — Study**
- Study nonmagical object/structure/vehicle/fortification/mechanism/equipment within 60 feet.
- Successful Intelligence check identifies plausible weakness/failure point.
- First attack/check exploiting it before end of Veteran's next turn gains Advantage.
- Cannot invent unsupported weaknesses.

## Change the Plan — current provisional level-3 combat feature

**Status:** Explicitly selected “for the moment.”

Current working wording:

> **Change the Plan**
>
> When one approach fails, you know how to turn the opening into something useful.
>
> Once during each **Attack action you take on your turn**, you can use one of the following responses:
>
> **Weapon to Unarmed.** Immediately after you miss a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can make an **Unarmed Strike** against that creature.
>
> **Unarmed to Weapon.** Immediately after a creature succeeds on its saving throw against the Grapple or Shove option of your Unarmed Strike, you can make one **melee weapon attack** against that creature.
>
> An attack or Unarmed Strike made by this feature can't trigger Change the Plan again.

### Current intentional ruling

The generated weapon attack currently retains **full normal Weapon Mastery access**.

Do not silently remove Mastery.

### Explicit playtest watchpoint

If the feature is too powerful, especially when paired with Weapon Mastery, the first proposed nerf is:

> The weapon attack generated by Change the Plan cannot use a Mastery property.

This is **not currently active**.

### Why this feature exists

It replaced a long sequence of less satisfying “bonus damage under condition X” ideas.

Its intended identity is:

- proper technique fails;
- Veteran immediately changes methods;
- weapon ↔ body;
- strike ↔ grapple/shove;
- practical adaptation rather than formal technique.

The 2024 Attack action already permits attacks with weapons or Unarmed Strikes, and Unarmed Strike is a normal attack option rather than a special “monk-only” mechanic.

Historical inspiration: Fiore de'i Liberi's armored sword material explicitly describes using cut, then thrust, then crossguard/pommel, and switching to grappling when sword attacks are ineffective.

## Veteran's Eye — current state

**Status:** UNSATISFACTORY / under active replacement review.

The old/current reference version contains:

1. one proficiency from:
   - History,
   - Investigation,
   - Perception,
   - Survival;
2. Field Assessment;
3. Size Up.

The user specifically said:

- a ribbon feature here is desirable;
- the problem is **word count/bloat, not excessive power**;
- Veteran's Eye feels like **two separate features**;
- **Size Up is a Study-action upgrade and therefore feels too much like a Veteran Lesson**.

Therefore:

### Do not assume Size Up survives.

The likely direction is to simplify Veteran's Eye radically, probably to **one compact fieldcraft ribbon** rather than multiple subfeatures.

Prior researched possibilities, none yet accepted:

**Condensed Field Assessment**
- Spend 1 minute examining battlefield/camp/route/structure/vehicle/equipment.
- Ask for one useful fact about condition, recent use, likely danger, or tactical value reasonably inferable from evidence.
- Could retain PB/LR.

**Old Campaigner**
- Skill proficiency from History/Investigation/Perception/Survival.
- Possibly artisan's-tool proficiency.
- Very compact but loses much distinctive fieldcraft.

**Seasoned Eye**
- Skill proficiency.
- Advantage in a narrow practical information domain involving battlefields, camps, routes, fortifications, equipment, signs of violence.

**Signs of Trouble**
- After 1 minute examining practical subject, automatically identify discernible evidence of recent use, damage, sabotage, or preparation for violence.
- Very small ribbon, potentially too campaign-dependent.

No option is canonical yet.

## Battlefield Readiness — accepted new level-7 direction

Old D.4.1 version allowed one choice:

- Take Position — move up to half Speed.
- Sweep — take Search.
- Assess — take Study.

The accepted new direction is:

> **At the start of the Veteran's first turn in each combat, choose two different Battlefield Readiness options instead of one.**

Existing options remain:

**Take Position**
- Move up to half Speed.

**Sweep**
- Take the Search action.

**Assess**
- Take the Study action.

At level 15:

**Brace**
- Gain Temporary Hit Points equal to twice PB.

Actions taken through Battlefield Readiness can benefit from a known Veteran Lesson that enhances that action.

Battlefield Readiness is once per combat.

### Important consequence

Vigilant Response is being removed; the stronger Battlefield Readiness occupies that level-7 power budget.

### Important Veteran's Eye dependency

If **Size Up is removed**, Assess loses its Veteran-specific creature-analysis application.

At Fighter 7–9 there is no Study Lesson available; Find the Weak Point does not unlock until 10.

This may mean players routinely choose **Take Position + Sweep** at 7–9.

Do not preserve Size Up merely to fix this. If Assess becomes too weak, reconsider Assess itself or eventually add/revise a Study Lesson.

## Been Here Before — level 10

**Status:** retained, but known structural concern remains.

### Recalled Lesson

When taking an action or Reaction that could benefit from a Veteran Lesson the Veteran does not know:

- may use an unknown qualified Lesson as though known;
- declare before required attack roll/ability check;
- cannot bypass/reset the Lesson's own use limits;
- once per Short or Long Rest.

### Change of Approach

After Short or Long Rest:
- replace one known Lesson with another qualified Lesson.

### Existing concern

Recalled Lesson may make niche Lessons **less likely to be permanently selected**, because players can learn evergreen Lessons and rely on Recalled Lesson when an unusual situation arises.

This was identified previously and remains unresolved.

Alternative preparation-based models were discussed but not adopted.

## No Lesson Wasted — level 15

**Status:** mostly retained, but currently incomplete because Finish the Wounded was replaced.

Current retained elements:

- after Short or Long Rest, replace **any number** of known Lessons with qualified Lessons;
- when Initiative is rolled, before first turn, choose one unknown qualified Lesson;
- know that temporary Lesson until combat ends;
- Battlefield Readiness gains **Brace: 2 × PB Temporary Hit Points**.

### Obsolete clause

The D.4.1/D.4.2 text says:

> Finish the Wounded increases to 2d10.

### Current hole

No accepted level-15 upgrade to **Change the Plan** exists yet.

Do not silently convert it into extra attacks, extra damage, additional uses, or full Mastery restrictions/benefits.

That is the next major combat-design decision after Veteran's Eye is resolved or in parallel with it.

## Veteran of a Hundred Fights — accepted level-18 replacement

The old D.4.1 capstone was rejected as **too bloated**, despite being acceptably powerful.

The accepted new concept is:

> **Veteran of a Hundred Fights.** At the start of each of your turns in combat, choose one creature you can see. Until the start of your next turn, you have Advantage on attack rolls against that creature and on saving throws it forces you to make, and its attack rolls against you have Disadvantage.

This is intended to be essentially the entire capstone.

### Important intended qualities

- proactive, not triggered by first being hit;
- one creature at a time;
- applies only to Veteran's attacks/saves and the chosen creature's attacks against Veteran;
- does not protect allies;
- does not grant another Reaction;
- does not require a Read Threat state;
- does not add special movement;
- does not rely on Vigilant Response;
- intentionally very strong against one high-priority opponent.

### Important interaction

Because the attack Advantage lasts until the start of the Veteran's next turn rather than only during the Veteran's turn, it currently also applies to off-turn attacks against that creature, such as:

- Hair Trigger;
- Hook and Turn;
- ordinary Opportunity Attacks.

This was recognized as a playtest watchpoint but **not preemptively restricted**.

If this is excessive, a possible later restriction is:

> Advantage on attack rolls against the creature **on your turn**.

Not currently accepted.

# 5. FINALIZED DECISIONS

## Decision: Keep Hard-Won Lessons

**Decision:**  
Keep Hard-Won Lessons and the current Lesson roster unchanged for the moment.

**Why:**  
It remains the main personalization engine and expresses practical specialization without requiring Battle Master-style superiority dice.

**Alternatives considered:**  
Various broader lesson categories, passive readiness lessons, more generic Veteran actions, package/archetype structures.

**Why alternatives were not chosen:**  
Mixing too many feature categories into Lessons risks hidden mandatory picks and mechanical comparison problems.

**Revisit only if:**  
Playtesting shows severe pick imbalance, excessive lookup burden, or that core features make many Lessons redundant.

---

## Decision: Replace Finish the Wounded

**Decision:**  
Finish the Wounded is superseded provisionally by **Change the Plan**.

**Why:**  
Finish the Wounded was powerful but boring. It asked almost no meaningful player decision once enemies had taken damage.

It also resembled the 2024 Hunter's **Colossus Slayer**, which likewise rewards attacks against creatures already missing Hit Points.

**Alternatives considered:**  
Take Their Measure, Commit to the Opening, Battle Footwork, Been Through Worse, Right Tool for the Fight, Learn the Pattern, mastery-sacrifice damage, Cheap Shot/Eye Gouge variants, weapon-assisted grappling, mixed attack sequencing.

**Why alternatives were not chosen:**  
See rejected-approaches section.

**Revisit only if:**  
Change the Plan proves mechanically excessive, too failure-dependent, or insufficient as a subclass combat feature.

---

## Decision: Preserve full Weapon Mastery on Change the Plan for initial testing

**Decision:**  
Follow-up weapon attacks generated by Change the Plan use Weapon Mastery normally.

**Why:**  
Feat/base-class synergies should not be preemptively removed merely because they are strong. The feature should first be tested in its most natural form.

**Important warning:**  
Graze and other Mastery interactions may make failure unusually productive.

**Revisit only if:**  
DPR/control testing shows meaningful excess.

**First nerf to test:**  
Generated weapon attack cannot use a Mastery property.

---

## Decision: Remove Vigilant Response

**Decision:**  
Vigilant Response is superseded.

**Why:**  
Although mechanically acceptable, it did not strongly express the Veteran theme and was too close in emotional/mechanical space to Berserker Barbarian's Retaliation.

The 2024 Berserker's Retaliation lets the Barbarian make a Reaction melee attack against a nearby creature after taking damage.

**Alternative chosen:**  
Strengthen Battlefield Readiness instead.

**Revisit only if:**  
Level 7 proves underpowered after playtesting.

---

## Decision: Strengthen Battlefield Readiness at level 7

**Decision:**  
Choose **two different options** rather than one.

**Why:**  
This strengthens the Veteran's most distinctive “already ready when combat begins” mechanic rather than adding an unrelated reaction-attack subsystem.

It also makes Brace more attractive at 15 because selecting Brace no longer necessarily consumes the Veteran's only opening benefit.

**Revisit only if:**  
Free movement + Search/Study combinations prove too much action compression or produce dominant mandatory selections.

---

## Decision: Simplify the level-18 capstone drastically

**Decision:**  
Replace old multi-part capstone with proactive one-creature mastery:

- Advantage attacking chosen creature.
- Advantage on saves it forces.
- Its attacks against Veteran have Disadvantage.
- Choose at start of each Veteran turn.
- Lasts to start of next Veteran turn.

**Why:**  
Old capstone was powerful enough but structurally bloated. It upgraded Battlefield Readiness, Finish the Wounded, Read the Threat, and Vigilant Response simultaneously.

The replacement expresses one clear high-level idea: the Veteran can instantly read one opponent.

**Revisit only if:**  
Solo-boss performance is oppressive or the feature makes too many other accuracy/defensive tools irrelevant.

# 6. WORKING ASSUMPTIONS

**Confidence:** High.

**Reason:**  
The feature occurs “once during each Attack action” and immediately generates the follow-up as part of that resolution.

This may matter for effects requiring an attack to occur “as part of the Attack action,” notably Grappler.

# 7. REJECTED / ABANDONED / SUPERSEDED APPROACHES

## Finish the Wounded

**Status:** Superseded.

**Reason:**  
Powerful but boring; trivial “missing any HP” condition; little player decision; somewhat overlaps Hunter Colossus Slayer identity.

**Do not re-propose unless:**  
The user explicitly asks to return to simple passive damage.

---

## Take Their Measure

Concept:
- extra damage after previously making an attack roll against the creature.

**Status:** Not accepted.

**Reason:**  
Still fundamentally a conditional damage rider, and user was not sold on this family of solutions.

**Do not re-propose unless:**  
Seeking intentionally simple damage mechanics again.

---

## Commit to the Opening

Concept:
- spend Reaction after a hit for extra damage.

**Status:** Not accepted.

**Reason:**  
Creates Reaction tension but remains “pay Reaction for damage,” and the project moved toward a more cohesive adaptive-fighting identity.

---

## Battle Footwork

Concept:
- small OA-safe movement after attacking.

**Status:** Not accepted.

**Reason:**  
Interesting tactical feature but did not capture the evolving “use whatever works” concept and overlapped somewhat with Break Contact/Tactical Shift.

---

## Been Through Worse

Concept:
- special Second Wind use when reduced to 0 HP.

**Status:** Not accepted.

**Reason:**  
Strong survivor flavor but highly swingy and not aligned with the emerging integrated weapon/unarmed fighting concept.

---

## Original Right Tool for the Fight

Concept:
- change prepared Weapon Mastery choice at Initiative.

**Status:** Rejected as specific implementation.

**Reason:**  
User liked the *theme* of “right tool” but not swapping to the objectively correct weapon.

The user reframed it as:

> **the tool you have is the best tool for the job**

Examples supplied:
- professional photographer using the phone already in their pocket instead of DSLR;
- mechanic hammering a Torx bit into a stripped hex fastener because it works.

This reframing directly led to unconventional weapon use and eventually Change the Plan.

---

## Mastery-for-damage only

Concept:
- forgo Mastery property → +1d10.

**Status:** Superseded as main concept.

**Reason:**  
Better than Finish the Wounded, but still not sufficiently interesting/unique by itself.

Useful conceptual residue:
- sacrificing formal technique for expedience remains thematically appropriate.

---

## Separate unarmed “just gets +damage” clause

**Status:** Rejected.

**Reason:**  
Would make weapon users make an interesting trade while unarmed users simply receive passive bonus damage.

---

## Eye Gouge / Cheap Shot as whole feature

Concept:
- low-damage special strike that Blinds briefly.

**Status:** Not selected.

**Reason:**  
Strong thematic ingredient but Blinded occupies significant power budget and risks becoming the obvious best choice.

Useful residue:
- the user liked the idea that Unarmed Strike includes headbutts, kicks, shoulder checks, etc., not just punches.
- the broader insight helped merge weapon/unarmed behavior into one combat identity.

---

## Weapon-assisted Grapple as sole feature

**Status:** Not selected.

**Reason:**  
Historically/thematically excellent but too melee/control-specific for the universal third-level feature.

---

## Improvised Sequence requiring alternating weapon/unarmed attacks

# 11. KEY RESEARCH / EVIDENCE / TEST RESULTS

## 2024 Fighter baseline

The Fighter has:

- Weapon Mastery at 1;
- Tactical Mind at 2;
- Tactical Shift at 5;
- Tactical Master at 9;
- Studied Attacks at 13;
- increasing Extra Attack count.

Tactical Master lets the Fighter substitute Push, Sap, or Slow when attacking with a weapon whose Mastery property the Fighter can use. Studied Attacks grants Advantage on the next attack roll against a creature after missing it.

**Implication:**  
Veteran features should not duplicate Tactical Master or accidentally assume Unarmed Strikes have Weapon Mastery.

[2024 Fighter overview on D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1746-2024-fighter-vs-2014-fighter-whats-new?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## Attack and Unarmed Strike rules

The 2024 Attack action allows a weapon attack or Unarmed Strike.

**Implication:**  
Change the Plan can naturally move between weapon attacks and Unarmed Strikes without inventing a new attack category.

[2024 Rules Glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## Grappler interaction

2024 Grappler's Punch and Grab permits a hit with an Unarmed Strike made as part of the Attack action on the character's turn to use both Damage and Grapple, once per turn.

**Potential Change the Plan implication:**  
Weapon miss → generated Unarmed Strike using Damage → if it hits and is considered part of the Attack action, Grappler could potentially add Grapple.

The anti-recursion clause prevents a subsequent failed Grapple generated by that follow-up from chaining back into another Change the Plan attack.

**Needs explicit playtest/rules review.**

---

## Berserker comparison

2024 Berserker Retaliation at level 10 grants a Reaction melee attack when taking damage from a creature within 5 feet.

**Implication:**  
Old Vigilant Response was not identical, but occupied similar mechanical territory. This supported removing it.

---

## Hunter Colossus Slayer comparison

2024 Hunter Colossus Slayer:
- +1d8 on a weapon hit against a creature missing Hit Points;
- once per turn.

**Implication:**  
Finish the Wounded was not merely “boring”; its fiction/mechanical condition was also already familiar territory.

---

## Historical martial precedent for Change the Plan

Fiore de'i Liberi's surviving material includes integrated sword, dagger, grappling, poleaxe, spear, and mounted techniques. In the armored sword material, Fiore explicitly describes:
- if cut is unavailable, thrust;
- if neither works, use crossguard/pommel;
- transition to grappling when advantageous.

**Implication:**  
Change the Plan's weapon ↔ unarmed transition is not merely cinematic invention; it maps well to historical integrated fighting systems.

[Fiore de'i Liberi — Sword in Armor (Wiktenauer)](https://www.wiktenauer.com/wiki/Fiore_de%27i_Liberi/Sword_in_Armor?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Important forthcoming Change the Plan interactions

### Graze

Potential sequence:

- Veteran misses with Graze weapon.
- Graze still produces its normal miss benefit.
- Change the Plan then grants an Unarmed Strike.

This makes the miss unusually productive.

**Status:** Explicit watchpoint, not currently considered automatically broken.

At Fighter 13:

- Studied Attacks grants Advantage on Veteran's next attack roll against target;

Do not preemptively remove unless playtesting shows a genuine problem.

### Grappler

Potential:

Strong synergy; test.

Because Change the Plan is once during **each Attack action on your turn**, Action Surge can potentially permit a second Change the Plan sequence.

This is likely intended under the current wording but should be explicitly tested.

# 13. TERMINOLOGY / GLOSSARY

**Veteran:**  
The Fighter subclass being designed.

**Hard-Won Lessons:**  
The level-3 feature granting selectable **Veteran Lessons**.

A self-contained selectable technique normally enhancing a specifically named action or Reaction.

**Evergreen Lesson:**  
A Lesson useful across many campaigns/builds rather than only in specialized environments.

Flavor concept, not currently a rules term. Includes headbutts, kicks, shoulder checks, pommel strikes, crossguard blows, eye gouges, etc.

# 14. RELATIONSHIPS / DEPENDENCIES

## Hard-Won Lessons ↔ Battlefield Readiness

Sweep and Assess are actual Search/Study actions and may benefit from Lessons that modify those actions.

Therefore:
- Sweep can use Threat Scan.
- Assess can eventually use Find the Weak Point when applicable.
- Veteran's Eye Size Up used to give Assess a creature-analysis use, but Size Up is now likely to be removed.

---

## Veteran's Eye ↔ Assess

Removing Size Up will reduce Assess's special utility at levels 7–9.

This is not automatically a reason to keep Size Up.

---

## Change the Plan ↔ base Fighter progression

Change the Plan naturally gains more opportunity to trigger as Fighter gains more attacks.

Important interactions:
- Weapon Mastery.
- Graze.
- Studied Attacks.
- Extra Attack.
- Action Surge.
- Grappler.
- potentially Great Weapon Master and other weapon-feat riders.

Because the generated attack is still an attack rather than a special damage packet, interactions need real optimization testing.

---

## Change the Plan ↔ Hair Trigger

Change the Plan is currently limited to an Attack action taken **on Veteran's turn**.

Hair Trigger usually executes a readied Attack action as a Reaction on another creature's turn.

Therefore Hair Trigger generally should **not** trigger Change the Plan.

Do not casually broaden Change the Plan to “whenever you take the Attack action” without recognizing this consequence.

---

## Removal of Vigilant Response ↔ Lessons

Removing the universal Reaction counterattack increases the relative value of:
- Hair Trigger;
- Hook and Turn;
- ordinary Opportunity Attacks.

This is considered beneficial because reaction offense becomes a specialization rather than default Veteran behavior.

---

## No Lesson Wasted ↔ niche Lessons

Full Short/Long Rest swapping and initiative temporary Lessons reduce the long-term commitment of Lesson choices.

This may make niche Lessons:
- easier to access when needed;
- but less likely to be permanently known.

This tension remains unresolved.

---

## Level-18 capstone ↔ Advantage sources

The accepted capstone gives Advantage against one chosen creature.

Therefore it overlaps with:
- Studied Attacks;
- Vex;
- Grappler's Advantage against grappled creatures;
- other Advantage sources.

This is expected and not automatically a flaw because Advantage does not stack numerically.

# 21. BOOTSTRAP SUMMARY FOR A FRESH CHAT

## Project

Designing a D&D 5.5e/2024 Fighter subclass called **Veteran**: a mundane, experienced soldier/campaigner/survivor who wins through fieldcraft, adaptability, practical violence, preparation, and knowing what works.

Do not turn it into Battle Master, Rogue, Ranger-without-magic, Cavalier/Sentinel, Eldritch Knight, or a Warlord.

## Current core

**Hard-Won Lessons** remains the main selectable system and is staying unchanged for now. Veteran knows 4/5/6/7/8 Lessons at Fighter 3/7/10/15/18. Current roster is 14 Lessons, modifying Dash, Hide, Dodge, Help, Search, Ready, Opportunity Attack, Influence, Magic-item activation, Utilize, and Study.

**Finish the Wounded is obsolete.** It was strong but boring and resembled a wounded-target damage rider.

Its provisional replacement is **Change the Plan**:

- once during each Attack action on Veteran's turn;
- after missing a creature with a melee weapon attack, immediately make an Unarmed Strike against it;
- after a creature succeeds against Veteran's Grapple or Shove Unarmed Strike, immediately make a melee weapon attack against it;
- generated attacks cannot trigger Change the Plan again.

**Important:** the generated weapon attack currently retains full Weapon Mastery. This is intentional for first testing. If too strong, first nerf to test is removing Mastery from that generated weapon attack.

Watch:
- Graze;
- Studied Attacks;
- Grappler;
- Action Surge;
- Extra Attack scaling.

**Veteran's Eye is unresolved.** User wants a compact ribbon but dislikes the current bloated package of proficiency + Field Assessment + Size Up. Size Up particularly feels wrong because it upgrades Study like a Lesson. Likely replacement should be a small fieldcraft/evidence-reading feature.

**Level 7:** Vigilant Response is obsolete. Strengthen **Battlefield Readiness** instead: at the start of first turn each combat, choose **two different options** from Take Position (half-Speed move), Sweep (Search), Assess (Study), with Brace (2 × PB THP) added at Fighter 15.

**Level 10 Been Here Before:** currently retained. Recalled Lesson allows one unknown qualified Lesson once per Short/Long Rest; rest swapping also exists. Concern remains that this reduces incentive to permanently know niche Lessons.

**Level 15:** No Lesson Wasted retains full rest-swapping, temporary initiative Lesson, and Brace. Its old Finish-the-Wounded-to-2d10 clause is now obsolete. There is **no accepted Change the Plan level-15 upgrade yet**.
