# Veteran
### Fighter Subclass — Iteration D.4.1

Veterans are fighters who have survived long enough to stop believing that battles are won cleanly.

They know which road invites an ambush, which wall will fail first, when an enemy is overcommitting, how to turn a table into cover, how to keep armor from rattling at the wrong moment, and when a wounded foe is one hard blow from collapse. Their skill comes neither from magic nor command. It comes from accumulated experience, ugly practicality, fieldcraft, and an unwillingness to make the same mistake twice.

A Veteran might have served in armies, militias, mercenary companies, caravan guards, border patrols, ship crews, or a hundred smaller fights no historian ever recorded. What matters is not rank or glory.

What matters is that they came back.

---

# Level 3: Hard-Won Lessons

Experience has left you with a repertoire of practical techniques, habits, tricks, remembered procedures, and hard-earned insights called **Veteran Lessons**.

You learn four Veteran Lessons of your choice from the Veteran Lesson list at the end of this subclass. You learn one additional Lesson when you reach Fighter levels 7, 10, 15, and 18.

| Fighter Level | Lessons Known |
|---|---:|
| 3 | 4 |
| 7 | 5 |
| 10 | 6 |
| 15 | 7 |
| 18 | 8 |

If a Lesson has a Fighter-level prerequisite, you must meet it to learn that Lesson.

Whenever you gain a Fighter level, you can replace one Veteran Lesson you know with another Lesson for which you qualify.

Unless a Lesson says otherwise, it modifies only the specific action or Reaction named in the Lesson and doesn't change that action into another action.

---

# Level 3: Finish the Wounded

You have learned to recognize the moment an injured enemy begins to fail.

Once during each **Attack action you take on your turn**, when you hit a creature with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike and the creature was missing any Hit Points immediately before the attack, you can deal an extra **1d10 damage** to it. The extra damage is a type dealt by the attack.

You can use this benefit only once during each Attack action. If you take another Attack action on the same turn, such as through Action Surge, you can use this benefit once during that action as well.

At Fighter level 15, the extra damage increases to **2d10**.

---

# Level 3: Veteran's Eye

Years of looking for danger have taught you what details matter.

Choose one of the following skills in which you lack proficiency: **History, Investigation, Perception, or Survival**. You gain proficiency in that skill.

Veteran's Eye also gives you the following benefits.

## Field Assessment

If you spend at least 1 minute examining a battlefield, campsite, roadway, building, fortification, vehicle, weapon, suit of armor, piece of adventuring gear, or similarly practical subject, you can ask the DM one of the following questions:

- Where is the strongest defensible position I can identify?
- Where is the most likely route of approach, withdrawal, or ambush?
- What here appears damaged, unstable, poorly maintained, or especially vulnerable to being disabled?
- What signs indicate recent passage, fighting, work, occupation, or disturbance?
- What practical equipment, labor, positioning, or route would most help overcome one visible obstacle here?

The DM gives you a truthful and useful answer based on evidence your character could reasonably notice or understand.

This feature doesn't reveal information for which there is no observable evidence and isn't supernatural detection.

You can use Field Assessment a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Size Up

When you take the **Study action**, you can study a creature you can see within 60 feet, using its appearance, equipment, behavior you have observed, and your knowledge of creatures of its kind as the source of knowledge.

The DM determines the applicable Intelligence check and DC as normal.

On a successful check, choose **two** of the following subjects:

**Defenses.** Armor, defensive habits, exposed areas, reliance on protection, or other observable defensive qualities.

**Movement.** Mobility, balance, reach, unusual locomotion, limitations, or movement habits.

**Attacks.** Favored weapons, likely reach, attack patterns, dangerous limbs, obvious preparation, or other observable offensive qualities.

**Physical State.** Injury, fatigue, instability, impaired movement, unusual vigor, or another useful indication of the creature's present physical state.

For each chosen subject, the DM tells you one truthful and useful qualitative fact that you could reasonably infer. The answer shouldn't merely repeat something already obvious unless that obvious fact has a useful tactical implication.

Size Up never reveals exact Armor Class, Hit Points, ability scores, saving-throw modifiers, attack bonuses, spell slots, legendary-resource counts, or a trait for which you have neither observed evidence nor relevant knowledge.

Once you succeed on the check to Size Up a creature, you can't use Size Up on that creature again until you finish a Long Rest.

# Level 7: Battlefield Readiness

You rarely need the opening moments of a fight to understand that a fight has begun.

At the start of your **first turn in each combat**, choose one of the following options.

## Take Position

You can immediately move up to half your Speed.

## Sweep

You immediately take the **Search action**.

## Assess

You immediately take the **Study action**.

An action taken through Battlefield Readiness can benefit from a Veteran Lesson you know that enhances that action.

You can use Battlefield Readiness only once per combat. It can't be repeated through Action Surge or another feature that grants you an additional action.

---

# Level 7: Vigilant Response

You have survived too many close fights to ignore an enemy who leaves itself exposed.

When a creature you can see within your reach misses you with an attack roll, you can use your Reaction to make one melee weapon attack or one Unarmed Strike against that creature.

This attack is a Reaction attack, not an Opportunity Attack.

You can use Vigilant Response only once between the start of one of your turns and the start of your next turn.

---

# Level 10: Been Here Before

Very few problems are truly new. Even when you haven't prepared specifically for the situation at hand, experience gives you something to fall back on.

You gain the following benefits.

## Recalled Lesson

When you take an action or Reaction that could be enhanced by a Veteran Lesson you don't know, you can choose to use one such Lesson as though you knew it, provided you meet its prerequisites.

You must choose the Lesson **before making any attack roll or ability check required by that action or Reaction**.

Using Recalled Lesson doesn't bypass or reset any limit on how often the chosen Lesson can be used.

Once you use Recalled Lesson, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

## Change of Approach

Whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can replace one Veteran Lesson you know with another Veteran Lesson for which you qualify.

---

# Level 15: No Lesson Wasted

Your experience has become broad enough that you can change your approach almost as quickly as circumstances change.

The extra damage from your **Finish the Wounded** feature increases to **2d10**.

Whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can replace any number of Veteran Lessons you know with other Veteran Lessons for which you qualify.

In addition, **when you roll Initiative and before your first turn begins**, choose one Veteran Lesson you don't know and for which you qualify. You know that Lesson until the combat ends.

Your Battlefield Readiness also gains the following option:

## Brace

You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to **twice your Proficiency Bonus**.

---

# Level 18: Veteran of a Hundred Fights

There are few mistakes left that someone hasn't already made in front of you.

Your Battlefield Readiness improves. When you use it, choose **three different options** rather than one. Resolve those options one at a time in any order.

You also gain the following benefits.

## Nothing New Under the Sun

Your Finish the Wounded feature no longer requires the target to be missing Hit Points.

All other limitations of Finish the Wounded still apply, including its limit of once during each Attack action you take on your turn.

## Read the Threat

Once between the start of one of your turns and the start of your next turn, immediately after a creature you can see hits you with an attack roll or you fail a saving throw caused by that creature, you can designate it as your **Read Threat**. No action is required.

Your Read Threat remains designated until the start of your next turn. You can have only one Read Threat at a time; designating another creature ends the previous designation.

When you designate your Read Threat, you can immediately move up to 10 feet. This movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks from your Read Threat.

While a creature is your Read Threat:

- its attack rolls against you have Disadvantage; and
- you have Advantage on saving throws it forces you to make.

## Punish the Mistake

When you use **Vigilant Response** against your Read Threat, make the attack with Advantage.

If the attack hits, it deals an extra **1d10 damage** of a type dealt by the attack.

This feature doesn't change the Reaction required to use Vigilant Response and doesn't give you an additional Reaction.

# Veteran Lessons

## Forced March — Dash

When you take the **Dash action**, you gain an additional 10 feet of movement for that action, and Difficult Terrain costs you no extra movement until the end of the current turn.

---

## Break Contact — Dash

When you take the **Dash action**, choose one creature you can see.

Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks from that creature for the rest of the current turn.

---

## Field Concealment — Hide

When you take the **Hide action**, Disadvantage imposed by armor you are wearing doesn't apply to the Dexterity (Stealth) check made as part of that action.

If the check succeeds, you can also choose a number of willing creatures up to your Proficiency Bonus that are within 30 feet of you and can see or hear you.

For the next 10 minutes, you and those creatures have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made as part of the Hide action, and Disadvantage imposed by armor doesn't apply to those checks.

This Lesson doesn't remove any other requirement of the Hide action.

Once you grant the 10-minute benefit of this Lesson, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

---

## Shield the Line — Dodge

When you take the **Dodge action**, choose one willing creature within 5 feet of you.

Until the start of your next turn, that creature has Half Cover while it remains within 5 feet of you and you aren't Incapacitated.

---

## Hold Ground — Dodge

When you take the **Dodge action**, you brace yourself until the start of your next turn.

During that time, you have Advantage on any saving throw or ability check you make to avoid being knocked Prone or moved against your will.

If an effect would move you against your will without allowing a saving throw or ability check, reduce the distance it moves you by 10 feet, to a minimum of 0 feet.

---

## Fire-Team Assist — Help

When you take the **Help action** to assist an ability check, the creature you help can be within 30 feet of you rather than adjacent to you, provided it can see or hear you.

When you take the Help action to assist an attack roll, the enemy can be within 15 feet of you rather than within 5 feet of you.

If the helped attack hits, the creature making that attack can immediately move up to 5 feet without provoking an Opportunity Attack from the creature it hit.

---

## Threat Scan — Search

When you take the **Search action** to locate a creature, you have Advantage on the Wisdom check if that creature has moved, made an attack, or made a clearly audible sound since the end of your last turn.

If the check succeeds, you also learn the direction from which that creature most recently moved or attacked, if that can reasonably be determined from the available evidence.

If you locate more than one creature with the same Search check, choose one of them. The first attack roll made against that creature by you or by a creature that can see or hear you before the start of your next turn has Advantage.

---

## Hair Trigger — Ready
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 7**

When you take the **Ready action** and prepare to take the Attack action with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike in response to your chosen trigger, you are especially dangerous when that trigger occurs.

If the trigger occurs and you use your Reaction to take that readied Attack action, you make **two attacks in total** as part of the action instead of one.

Starting at Fighter level 15, you make **three attacks in total** instead.

Once during that readied Attack action, when you hit with one of its attacks, you can deal an extra **1d10 damage** of a type dealt by that attack.

---

## Hook and Turn — Opportunity Attack
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 7**

When you hit a creature with an **Opportunity Attack**, the attack deals an extra **1d10 damage** of a type dealt by the attack.

In addition, if the creature is Large or smaller, you can move it up to 5 feet to an unoccupied space within your reach.

This movement doesn't require the creature to move directly away from you.

---

## Common Soldier's Tongue — Influence
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 10**

When you take the **Influence action** to influence a creature whose life or profession gives it substantial experience with military service, guard duty, mercenary work, dangerous manual labor, caravan work, shipboard work, or another comparable practical occupation, you have Advantage on the ability check if your approach concerns a subject such as:

- danger or local threats;
- equipment or supplies;
- routes or travel conditions;
- pay or working conditions;
- security;
- fortifications;
- logistics; or
- practical survival.

This Lesson doesn't magically compel a creature, change what it is willing to do, or allow an Influence attempt that would otherwise be impossible.

---

## Battlefield Activation — Magic
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 10**

When you take the **Magic action specifically to activate a magic item**, including a magic item whose activation causes it to cast a spell, you can draw or stow that item as part of the action.

After the item's activation resolves, you can make one attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action before the end of the current turn.

This Lesson doesn't allow you to use a magic item whose requirements you don't meet, doesn't change the action required to activate the item, and doesn't allow you to use the Magic action through Action Surge.

---

## Sabotage — Utilize
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 10**

When you take the **Utilize action** to disable, jam, wedge, tangle, break, or obstruct a nonmagical object or mechanism, you have Advantage on any ability check required to do so.

If you succeed and your work could reasonably be reversed rather than requiring actual repairs, a creature attempting to reverse it must take an action and succeed on an ability check against a DC equal to:

**8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier**, chosen when you perform the sabotage.

A creature automatically succeeds if it possesses an obvious means of bypassing your work, such as the proper key for a lock you merely jammed from one side.

---

## Quick Barricade — Utilize
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 10**

When you take the **Utilize action** while adjacent to sturdy movable objects or suitable loose material, you can arrange that material into a temporary barricade along one edge of a 5-foot space within your reach.

The material must be substantial enough to obstruct attacks, such as a table, door, several crates, heavy furniture, shields fixed into position, rubble, or comparable material.

Until the barricade is moved, destroyed, or otherwise rendered ineffective, a creature behind it has Half Cover against attacks and effects that pass through the protected edge.

The barricade gains no special durability from this Lesson.

---

## Find the Weak Point — Study
**Prerequisite: Fighter level 10**

When you take the **Study action** to examine a nonmagical object, structure, vehicle, fortification, mechanism, or piece of equipment you can see within 60 feet, you can look specifically for a point of failure.

On a successful Intelligence check, the DM identifies one plausible weakness, damaged component, poorly supported section, vulnerable mechanism, or other practical point that could help someone damage, disable, force, or bypass the subject.

Until the end of your next turn, the first attack roll or ability check made by you or by a creature that can see or hear your explanation to exploit that weakness is made with Advantage.

This Lesson can't identify a weakness that isn't supported by the subject's construction, condition, or other evidence available to you.
