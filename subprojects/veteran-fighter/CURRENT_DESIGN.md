# Veteran Fighter current design

**Working reference:** [Public Test 1 — complete rules](versions/public-test-1-2026-10-05.md), preserved without mechanical editing. Its introductory “recommended/proposed release” status is retained; this audit does not declare it finally approved.

| Fighter level | Public Test 1 feature package |
|---|---|
| 3 | Hard-Won Lessons; Practical Experience; Practical Fighting (Practical Contact, Close Work, Hit or Correct) |
| 7 | Battlefield Readiness: two different choices; Counter-Ambush, Take Position, Read the Field, Brace |
| 10 | Been Here Before: Change of Approach and limited Reserve Lessons |
| 15 | Still in the Fight: Reserve Readiness, Dig Deep, Improved Brace |
| 18 | Veteran of a Hundred Fights: one visible threat selected at each turn's start |

There are 13 base selections, 2 level-7 selections and 5 level-10 selections. Battlefield Improvisation contains Deliver/Misdirect; Fire-Team Assist contains Distract/Two-Man Takedown. Attack Procedures use bounded ordinary attack replacements; Public Test 1 does not give the later experimental Procedure→Follow-Up permission.

Use the complete source for eligibility, costs, timing, equipment and recursion limits. The [October 7 audit](archive/mechanical-audit-2026-10-07.md) examines this build and recommends instrumented testing rather than an automatic numerical nerf. The [October 5 current-state report](archive/current-state-2026-10-05.md) preserves intent and history but predates the testing release's consolidations.

**Experimental:** [A/A.1 and proposed Revision 2 handoff](experiments/two-lane-and-revision-2-handoff.md). These are separate alternatives; complete rules are missing. Approval to explore them does not replace this reference.
