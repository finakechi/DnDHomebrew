# Horizon Walker 2024 current design

Use [Playtest 0.2 Section 5](versions/playtest-0.2-2026-10-15.md#5-complete-player-facing-rules) for the complete current working rules. This pointer avoids maintaining a second full mechanics copy.

| Ranger level | Working package |
|---|---|
| 3 | Horizon Walker Spells, Planar Resonance, independent non-stacking Planar Warrior, resource-funded Folded Step |
| 7 | Ethereal Step and improved movement/fallback |
| 11 | Improved planar damage; capped Distant Strike teleports and multi-target extra attack |
| 13 | Relentless Focus replaces Relentless Hunter; planar investigation improvement |
| 15 | Spectral Defense with one-event protection and repositioning |
| 17 | Planar precision replaces Precise Hunter |
| 20 | Planar Mastery replaces Foe Slayer, preserving an optional Mark use |

The level-13/17/20 replacements are mandatory **within this proposed integrated version**, not additional bonuses to the published features and not general changes to other Rangers. The full document governs spell choices, costs, refreshes and eligibility.

[V1.0](versions/revised-report-v1.0-2026-10-15.md) and [0.1](versions/playtest-0.1-2026-10-15.md) preserve different approaches. Report section 4 also retains transformed-Mark, independent-only, full-replacement and hybrid alternatives. Do not mix them into one build.
