# Horizon Walker

**Comprehensive 2024 Revision**

*Research, redesigned rules, balance review, and playtesting framework*

**Version 1.0 | 15 October 2026**

> **Design position:** A planar investigator and spatial combatant with a viable alternative to Hunter's Mark—without rewriting the 2024 Ranger.

Prepared as a consolidated report from the preceding Horizon Walker homebrew design. Player-facing rules are collected in Part III; analysis and optional variants are outside the rules text.

Unofficial homebrew. Requires the 2024 Ranger class rules and DM approval.

## Executive Summary

This revision preserves the original Horizon Walker's distinctive Force damage, short planar phasing, attack-linked teleportation, and reactive defense. It expands the narrow Detect Portal into an investigative toolkit, eliminates Planar Warrior's recurring Bonus Action tax, and keeps the subclass compatible with the 2024 Ranger at levels 3, 7, 11, and 15.

| **Design question**                         | **V1.0 solution**                                                                                               |
|---------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **How to avoid mandatory Hunter's Mark?**   | Planar Warrior adds no-action, once-per-turn Force damage; it cannot stack with Hunter's Mark damage.           |
| **How to make planar awareness useful?**    | Liminal Attunement provides passive sensing, a limited large-area survey, and close examination.                |
| **What preserves Horizon Walker identity?** | Ethereal Step, Distant Strike's pre-attack teleports, and a dimensional defensive Reaction.                     |
| **How does high-level scaling work?**       | Planar Warrior gains non-Mark concentration support at 13, first-attack Advantage at 17, and 2d10 damage at 20. |

## Part I — Research Findings and Original Subclass Assessment

### 2024 Ranger Context

The 2024 Ranger retains Weapon Mastery and Extra Attack at level 5 and gains mobility, expertise, durability, and higher-level sensory abilities through its base-class progression. Crucially, several features promote repeated use of Hunter's Mark: Favored Enemy (level 1), Relentless Hunter (13), Precise Hunter (17), and Foe Slayer (20).

Hunter's Mark spends a Bonus Action to start, requires concentration, and spends another Bonus Action when the spell is transferred after a marked enemy drops to 0 Hit Points. It rewards repeated attacks against one target. This makes alternative concentration spells costly and creates friction with subclass Bonus Actions.

### Assessment of the Original Horizon Walker

| **Feature**              | **Existing strengths / problems**                                             | **Treatment**        |
|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Horizon Walker Magic** | Appropriate planar and movement spells, but Haste competes for concentration. | Selective revision   |
| **Detect Portal**        | Strong flavor; little value in adventures with no conventional portals.       | Major replacement    |
| **Planar Warrior**       | Force conversion and bonus damage; every-turn Bonus Action and 30-foot limit. | Rebuild              |
| **Ethereal Step**        | Memorable infiltration, but originally limited by Etherealness interaction.   | Refine               |
| **Distant Strike**       | Signature teleport-before-attack; extra attack requires three different foes. | Preserve and clarify |
| **Spectral Defense**     | Reliable defensive Reaction; narrower than some modern defenses.              | Enhance modestly     |

### Key Diagnosis

**Combat friction.** The original Planar Warrior asks a Ranger to spend a Bonus Action every turn for once-per-turn damage. This conflicts with Hunter's Mark, Misty Step, two-weapon routines, and Nature's Veil. Its 30-foot restriction also discourages archery at normal ranges.

**Exploration inconsistency.** The original Detect Portal locates the closest planar portal within one mile once per rest, which can go unused for entire campaigns. A planar explorer should also understand traces, local overlaps, anomalous spaces, and the hazards of portals without becoming a universal magical detector.

**What already works.** Distant Strike's three-target requirement turns space into a tactical puzzle and prevents a free third attack against bosses. Ethereal Step and Spectral Defense anchor the evasive, otherworldly identity and need improvements rather than wholesale replacement.

### Comparative Official Archetypes

The 2024 Hunter is an important baseline for consistent once-per-turn damage and versatile defense. Fey Wanderer shows how supernatural identity can support knowledge and social play; Gloom Stalker supplies stronger ambush-oriented tools. The redesigned Horizon Walker targets mobility and planar investigation as its specialties, not the highest possible single-target damage.

## Part II — Major Design Decisions

### Independent Planar Warrior, Mutually Exclusive with Hunter's Mark

Planar Strike adds once-per-turn Force damage when a weapon attack hits, with no action, concentration, slot expenditure, or daily pool. This lets the Ranger reserve their Bonus Action for teleportation and utility and concentrate on spells other than Hunter's Mark.

It cannot be used while concentrating on Hunter's Mark, nor on a turn after dealing that spell's extra damage. Thus a Ranger can choose sustained marked-target offense or flexible planar offense but cannot simply stack both damage engines.

At levels 13 and 17, parallel benefits keep the non-Mark path viable as the base class adds Mark-specific rewards. At 20, the planar damage die increases to 2d10. All are subclass features, requiring no base-class house rules.

| **Candidate solution**           | **Trade-off**                                                              | **Decision**             |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Allow full Mark stacking**     | Adds essentially free damage to an already strong repeating damage source. | Reject                   |
| **Make Mark concentration-free** | Broadly buffs Ranger options, potentially beyond subclass scope.           | Reject                   |
| **Count a new feature as Mark**  | Causes complex interactions with level 17 and 20 features.                 | Reject                   |
| **Rewrite base Ranger features** | Direct solution, but sacrifices drop-in compatibility.                     | Optional house rule only |
| **Separate non-stacking strike** | Preserves class choice, costs no extra action, and stays easy to run.      | Select                   |

### Liminal Attunement as Layered Investigation

Passive awareness gives the Ranger a recurring supernatural identity. Planar Survey offers powerful directional exploration at a limited frequency. Planar Study yields detailed, conditional information from a nearby known phenomenon. These layers distinguish sensing, locating, and understanding.

The feature intentionally does not detect creatures solely because they are extraplanar; it cannot determine exact teleported destinations, reveal secret passwords, automatically solve planar hazards, or replicate unrestricted Detect Magic, Locate Creature, or Plane Shift.

### Preserving Deliberate Constraints

Distant Strike retains its three-distinct-target condition. Spatial Assault does not allow free teleportation by attacking harmless scenery. Ethereal Step is a brief self-contained dimensional shift usable on all planes, but with a limited rest-based resource and displacement damage if reappearing in a solid space. Spectral Defense keeps its Reaction cost.

## Part III — Complete Revised Horizon Walker Subclass

The following section is intended for players and DMs. It contains the complete revised subclass rules, separate from designer commentary.

### Horizon Walker | Ranger Subclass

*Guardian of the Boundaries Between Worlds*

The planes of existence are not isolated realms. Their boundaries bend, intersect, and sometimes fracture, allowing creatures and strange forces to pass between worlds.

Horizon Walkers dedicate themselves to understanding and protecting these boundaries. Through unusual perception, specialized knowledge, and disciplined manipulation of planar energy, they identify disturbances in the fabric of reality, pursue threats across dimensional thresholds, and move through spaces that would obstruct ordinary travelers.

In combat, a Horizon Walker can infuse their attacks with planar force, vanish momentarily between worlds, and turn distance itself into an advantage.

| **Ranger level** | **Features**                                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| **3**            | Horizon Walker Spells; Liminal Attunement; Planar Warrior |
| **7**            | Ethereal Step                                             |
| **11**           | Distant Strike                                            |
| **15**           | Spectral Defense                                          |

Planar Warrior also improves at Ranger levels 11, 13, 17, and 20 as stated in its rules.

### Level 3: Horizon Walker Spells

Your connection to the multiverse grants you additional magic. When you reach a Ranger level specified in the Horizon Walker Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spell prepared. These spells count as Ranger spells for you and don't count against the number of Ranger spells you can prepare.

| **Ranger level** | **Spell**                     |
|------------------|-------------------------------|
| **3**            | Protection from Evil and Good |
| **5**            | Misty Step                    |
| **9**            | Blink                         |
| **13**           | Banishment                    |
| **17**           | Teleportation Circle          |

You cast these spells using your normal Ranger spellcasting rules, including any required spell slots and components.

### Level 3: Liminal Attunement

Your senses have become attuned to the boundaries separating the planes of existence. You perceive disturbances in the fabric of reality that others would overlook, and you have learned to interpret their significance.

**Planar Knowledge.** You gain proficiency in the Arcana skill. If you already have that proficiency, you instead gain proficiency in Investigation or Nature (your choice). Whenever you make an Arcana check concerning the planes of existence, portals, teleportation, extradimensional spaces, or planar phenomena, you can use Wisdom instead of Intelligence for the check.

**Liminal Awareness.** While you are conscious, you magically sense the presence of planar phenomena within 30 feet of yourself. For this feature, a planar phenomenon is one of the following:

- A magically sustained portal or gateway connecting different planes, including an inactive gateway that retains a magical connection.

- A breach, rift, or region where two planes overlap or their boundaries are unstable.

- An active opening into an extradimensional space.

- A lingering spatial disturbance created by teleportation or travel between planes within the past 10 minutes.

When one or more such phenomena are within range, you know that at least one is present. This awareness doesn't reveal the phenomenon's exact location, number, nature, or associated plane.

Additionally, whenever a creature teleports or travels between planes, you sense that transportation occurred if its departure or arrival point is on your current plane and within 60 feet of you. You don't automatically learn which creature traveled, where it departed from, or where it arrived.

Your awareness doesn't reveal the location or creature type of an extraplanar creature merely because it originates from another plane.

**Planar Survey.** As a Magic action, you can extend your senses and survey the surrounding area. When you do so, you detect the nearest qualifying phenomenon of each of the following categories, provided it is on your current plane:

| **Phenomenon**                                     | **Survey range** |
|----------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| **Planar portal, gateway, or breach**              | 1 mile           |
| **Planar overlap or spatial instability**          | 300 feet         |
| **Active extradimensional opening**                | 300 feet         |
| **Lingering teleportation or planar-travel trace** | 300 feet         |

For each category containing a detectable phenomenon, you learn its general direction, whether it is above or below you, and which of these distance bands contains it: within 30 feet, 31–300 feet, or 301 feet–1 mile. You learn no exact location or destination through this survey.

Once you use Planar Survey, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

**Planar Study.** As an action, you can examine a planar phenomenon or lingering transportation trace within 10 feet of you whose location you have discovered. Make a DC 15 Arcana check, using Planar Knowledge if applicable.

On a successful check, you learn the following information, to the extent that the phenomenon possesses discernible characteristics:

- Whether it is a portal, a spatial disturbance, a planar overlap, or a transportation remnant.

- Whether its magical structure appears stable, unstable, or deteriorating.

- The associated plane, if its characteristics correspond to a recognizable planar signature.

You can also identify one magical or environmental hazard directly associated with the phenomenon, if such a hazard is discernible.

This examination doesn't reveal secret passwords, hidden activation conditions, a portal's exact destination, or the identity or current location of a teleported creature.

Once you examine a particular phenomenon using this feature, you can't examine that same phenomenon again until you finish a Short or Long Rest, regardless of whether the check succeeds.

**Limitations.** Liminal Attunement detects planar phenomena, not magic generally. It doesn't reveal ordinary spells, inactive extradimensional containers whose openings are closed, or creatures merely because they were summoned or originate from another plane.

The feature can sense phenomena through ordinary nonmagical barriers, but it can't detect through lead barriers or into areas protected from Divination magic. It also doesn't function inside an Antimagic Field.

Magic that specifically conceals a phenomenon from Divination magic prevents its detection by this feature. The feature doesn't itself dispel, penetrate, or identify such concealment.

A phenomenon's detectable characteristics are determined by its actual nature. A successful check cannot reveal information that the phenomenon doesn't contain.

### Level 3: Planar Warrior

You have learned to draw upon the energy existing between the planes, channeling it through your attacks to strike beyond conventional defenses.

**Planar Strike.** Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with an attack roll using a weapon, you can empower that attack with planar energy. When you do so, the weapon's damage dealt by the attack becomes Force damage instead of its normal damage type, and the target takes an extra 1d8 Force damage.

This extra damage increases to 2d8 when you reach Ranger level 11 and 2d10 when you reach Ranger level 20.

Using Planar Strike requires no action, spell slot, or concentration, and you can use it without expending a resource.

You can't use Planar Strike while concentrating on Hunter's Mark. You also can't use Planar Strike on a turn during which you have already dealt extra damage with Hunter's Mark.

**Planar Concentration (Ranger Level 13).** You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws you make to maintain concentration on a Ranger spell other than Hunter's Mark.

**Dimensional Precision (Ranger Level 17).** While you aren't concentrating on Hunter's Mark, you have Advantage on the first attack roll you make with a weapon on each of your turns.

### Level 7: Ethereal Step

You have learned to slip briefly into the space between planes, passing through obstacles as though the boundaries of the world were no longer fixed.

As a Bonus Action, you can shift yourself and the objects you are wearing or carrying into a temporary space between planes. The effect lasts until the end of your current turn. While shifted, you gain the following benefits:

**Incorporeal Passage.** You can move through creatures and objects on the plane you departed as if they were Difficult Terrain.

**Unbound Movement.** You gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed for the duration.

**Planar Separation.** You can perceive your original plane, although your vision into it is limited to 60 feet and appears in shades of gray.

You can't affect or be affected by creatures, objects, or effects on your original plane while shifted, unless a feature specifically permits interaction between planes. Creatures on that plane similarly can't perceive you without an ability that permits such perception.

When the effect ends, you return to your original plane at your current location. If that location is occupied by a creature or solid object, you instead appear in the nearest unoccupied space you can occupy, and you take Force damage equal to twice the number of feet you were displaced.

This feature works on any plane of existence. Your temporary dimensional state doesn't permit travel to another permanent plane or interaction with creatures on unrelated planes.

Once you use Ethereal Step, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

### Level 11: Distant Strike

Distance has become an obstacle you can overcome with a thought. Your attacks carry you through brief dimensional passages, allowing you to appear wherever your enemies least expect you.

**Spatial Assault.** Immediately before each attack you make with a weapon as part of the Attack action, you can teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

You can use this teleportation before each eligible attack made with that action, including additional attacks granted by weapon properties or other features. You must be attacking a creature to use this teleportation; attacks against objects don't qualify.

**Distant Strike.** When you attack at least two different creatures with an Attack action, you can make one additional attack with a weapon as part of that same action, provided the additional attack targets a third creature you haven't attacked with that action. The additional attack can benefit from Spatial Assault.

You can gain no more than one additional attack per Attack action from this feature.

### Level 15: Spectral Defense

You can momentarily displace your physical form from the path of an attack, allowing its force to pass through the spaces between your body and reality.

When you take damage from an attack, you can use your Reaction to gain Resistance to all damage dealt by that attack. Immediately after that damage is resolved, you can teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

This teleportation requires no additional action or resource. You can use this feature whenever you have a Reaction available.

### Compatibility Checks and Limiting Interactions

- Levels 13, 17, 20: official Hunter's Mark class features still work whenever the spell is used. The independent Planar Warrior path gains its separate benefits instead.

- Weapon Mastery (Nick): a Light-weapon extra attack can increase attack and teleport count, but cannot increase Planar Strike's once-per-turn damage.

- Action Surge: another Attack action can qualify for another Distant Strike extra attack, if its three-target rule is satisfied; Planar Strike still occurs once per turn.

- Haste: the special additional action allows only one attack and therefore cannot independently satisfy Distant Strike's three-distinct-target trigger.

- Misty Step and Nature's Veil: Bonus Action competition remains a tactical decision, not an every-round mandatory tax.

- Spectral Defense: damage reduction applies to attacks, not to every saving-throw damage effect. It consumes the Reaction.

- Planar Survey: detects signatures and directional bands, not precise destinations, identities, activation keys, or unrestricted magic.

### Expected Strengths and Weaknesses

Best environments: mobile combats with several foes, dungeons containing magical movement or extradimensional features, investigative arcs, and adventuring days with resource scarcity. Less favorable environments: single-target damage races, adventures without planar phenomena, low-action campaigns in which short teleportation cannot change positioning, and combats dominated by area-effect saving throw damage.

## Part V — Design Rationale and Change Log

| **Feature**               | **Preserved**                             | **Changed and why**                                                                                     |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Horizon Walker Spells** | Five always-prepared thematic spells      | Replace Haste with Blink to reduce concentration pressure and highlight planar phasing.                 |
| **Detect Portal**         | Knowledge of planar thresholds            | Replace with Liminal Attunement; broaden from portals to transport traces, overlaps, and investigation. |
| **Planar Warrior**        | Force damage and level scaling            | No Bonus Action or 30-foot cap; mutual exclusivity with Mark limits stacking.                           |
| **Ethereal Step**         | Brief phasing for infiltration            | Independent dimensional state works on any plane with explicit limits.                                  |
| **Distant Strike**        | Teleport before attacks; attack third foe | Clarify Attack action, creatures, eligible additional attacks, and target requirement.                  |
| **Spectral Defense**      | Reaction-based Resistance                 | Adds 10-foot retreat after the attack resolves.                                                         |

2024-specific motivations: Bonus Action friction with Hunter's Mark, scaling against levels 13/17/20, Weapon Mastery interactions, and terminology such as prepared spells and Magic actions.

Original-design motivations: an infrequently useful Detect Portal, brittle Ethereal Plane dependencies, and a defensive feature that could better express spatial magic.

The spell revision deliberately retains Protection from Evil and Good, Misty Step, Banishment, and Teleportation Circle. Not granting Plane Shift preserves a distinction between recognizing planar gateways and being able to traverse the multiverse at will.

## Part VI — Optional Alternatives and Playtesting

### Optional Alternatives

**Alternative A — Haste rather than Blink.** Use the original 9th-level Horizon Walker spell, Haste, if a group prefers offensive tempo and the risk of losing concentration to the stronger planar flavor and non-concentration defense of Blink. This is a single-spell substitution, not a required rules change.

**Alternative B — Base-class redesign.** A table that wants to remove Hunter's Mark dependency for all Rangers may redesign Relentless Hunter, Precise Hunter, and Foe Slayer across subclasses. This is intentionally outside the default Horizon Walker; universal substitutions have uneven interactions with Hunter, Beast Master, and other builds.

### Playtest Matrix

| **Scenario**                               | **What to observe**                                                                        |
|--------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Single durable enemy**                   | At levels 3/7/11/17/20 compare Planar Warrior to Hunter's Mark over 4–5 rounds.            |
| **Three to five scattered enemies**        | Check Distant Strike targeting, line of sight, longbow vs. melee, and Nick teleport count. |
| **Six-encounter day with two Short Rests** | Track Ethereal Step and Survey uses, concentration choice, and slot consumption.           |
| **Hidden portal / fresh teleport trace**   | Test whether Liminal Attunement creates leads without revealing exact answers.             |
| **Dungeons and non-Ethereal planes**       | Test Ethereal Step through obstacles, planar walls, and magical countermeasures.           |
| **Level-15+ mixed damage**                 | Compare Spectral Defense against attacks, multiattacks, and saving-throw spells.           |

### Metrics to Record

- Rounds, attack attempts, successful hits, DPR from subclass features, and total damage by encounter.

- Bonus Actions left available for repositioning, stealth, and spells; concentration spell actually selected.

- Times that detection yielded actionable information versus mere notification that magic was present.

- Teleports that altered reachable enemies, cover access, hazard avoidance, or line of sight.

- Time spent adjudicating unusual planar phenomena, protection effects, or temporary phasing.

### Readiness Assessment

V1.0 is complete enough for controlled table playtesting with the published 2024 Ranger. The recommended first iteration is the non-stacking Planar Warrior implementation. Test exploration frequency and tactical mobility before increasing damage. Priority areas for iteration are Liminal Attunement's clarity in varied campaigns, the interaction of many pre-attack teleports with battlefield terrain, and the high-level appeal of the non-Mark combat path.
