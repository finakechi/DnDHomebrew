# Horizon Walker 2024

A modernization of Xanathar's planar Ranger for the 2024 Ranger. The brief prioritizes independence from Hunter's Mark, useful planar investigation, early spatial identity and recognizable phasing/teleport/defense features.

[Playtest 0.2](versions/playtest-0.2-2026-10-15.md) is the current working reference designated by the visible October 15 summary request. It is a complete proposed playtest design, with integrated Ranger feature replacements; the designation does not approve every mechanic or establish table balance. Older designs remain available for comparison.
