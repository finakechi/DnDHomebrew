# Rules references

This is a register of sources found in recovered work, not a fresh certification of current errata. Rules claims in archived reports retain their original research dates. Reopen official/current sources when a new design decision depends on them.

| Reference | Location or recovery state | Intended use and limit |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 / revised 5e baseline | User's explicit target; report bibliographies | Default for Veteran, Bartitsu and Horizon Walker; label legacy material individually |
| SRD 5.2.1 | Attachment `SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf`, `libfile_6da7d6d5ef90819197f2dee8a6d229f5`, discovered by search; contents not audited in this run | Open rules reference already cited by reports; version currency not independently checked here |
| 2024 PHB/Basic Rules/errata | Existing subclass audit source registers | Verify exact current wording before changing mechanics; do not substitute a 2014 transcription |
| Xanathar's Horizon Walker | Original chapter access gap documented by Horizon reports | Original mechanics researched through attributed sources; no claim of complete paid-book access |
| Shadowdark Quickstart | External repository's [inventory](https://github.com/finakechi/Shadowdark/blob/main/docs/research/quickstart-inventory.md) | External project holds its original guides/assets; this audit did not reread every guide |
| Bartitsu history | [Recovered historical report](../subprojects/bartitsu-rogue/archive/historical-foundations.md) | Historical/design inspiration; its old-edition mechanical discussion does not govern 2024 rules |
| Third-party designs and community feedback | Each report's bibliography; raw Veteran feedback in its asset index | Leads and perspectives, not official rules or proof of table balance |

For future verification, record edition, publication/version date, access date, exact section/page, authority type, accessible scope and unresolved interpretation. Separate official text, licensed reproduction, unofficial transcription, community analysis and original homebrew. Do not copy paid sourcebooks into this public repository.

Corpses/objects, Blindsight and Ranger-specific spells are pending reference work in [research notes](../subprojects/build-and-rules-research/STATUS.md); no answer is fabricated here.
