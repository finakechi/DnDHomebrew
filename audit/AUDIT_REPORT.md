## Major findings

1. The designated repository and Drive folder are newly initialized rather than existing archives. Their emptiness does not imply that creative work is absent elsewhere.
2. File upload dates and suffixes are unreliable version markers. Verified duplicate groups include Veteran handoff/timeline/transcript/audit/context files and all four Horizon Walker 0.1 copies.
3. Veteran's old broad attack-replacement rejection must not be misread as forbidding every later bounded Procedure experiment. The user's A/A.1 choices apply within that experiment, not automatically to Public Test 1.
4. Horizon 0.2's reference to identical “(1)/(2)/(3)” files concerns 0.1 copies, not three distinct 0.2 revisions.
5. Bartitsu's balance report recommends tuning but does not establish user adoption. Its originality report contains rules errors already identified in the balance report; neither establishes legal clearance or proven uniqueness.
6. Hearth & Hazard and Shadowdark have different rule baselines. Their exploration or character rules must not be silently merged.
7. Shadowdark main is not sufficient for the latest twelve-class work; PR #1 must be checked until merged or replaced.
8. A complete Amadeus monster stat block and the full later Veteran experimental texts were not recovered. Memory summaries are insufficient to recreate exact numbers or wording.
